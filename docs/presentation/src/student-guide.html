Getting started

Create your account and choose your courses. It takes a couple of minutes.

Create your account

  1. Open the sign-up pageGo to smart-exam-timetable.vercel.app/register on your phone or computer.
  2. Enter your detailsFull Name, Matric Number (for example F/ND/24/3210001; capitals are added for you), Email, then your Department → Programme → Level.
  3. Choose a passwordType it in Password and again in Confirm Password, then tap Create account. You are signed in straight away.

Choose your courses

  1. Select Your CoursesRight after sign-up you see the courses for your level and the current semester, all ticked.
  2. Untick any electives you are not takingThe counter shows how many courses are selected.
  3. Tap Complete EnrollmentYou are taken to your timetable. (Skip for now goes there too; you can enrol later on the Courses tab.)

Finding your way around

Four tabs, on your phone or on a computer.

Sign in at /login with your email and password. The portal has four tabs. On a phone they sit in a bar along the bottom of the screen; on a computer they are in a bar across the top, together with the light/dark switch and Sign out.

TabWhat you find there
TimetableYour published exams: what is next (with a countdown), what is coming up and what is completed, with hall and seat.
NavigateEvery exam hall on campus, each with a map and live walking directions.
CoursesThe courses for your level this semester: enrol or drop, and see your total credit units.
ProfileYour details, your avatar, the light/dark switch and Sign out.
Tip

Bookmark the portal, or add it to your home screen from your browser's menu, so it is one tap away on exam day. The portal opens in light mode. If you prefer dark mode, switch it on the Profile tab.

Signing in next time

  1. Open the portalGo to smart-exam-timetable.vercel.app and you are taken to the sign-in page.
  2. Enter your email and passwordThen tap Sign in. You go straight to your Timetable.
  3. Following a link?If you opened a link to a particular page, you are taken back to it after signing in.

On a phone

The same four tabs sit along the bottom of the screen. The highlighted tab shows where you are.

3

Your exam timetable

Everything you need for each paper, on one screen.

Your timetable appears as soon as the exams office publishes it. Use the session selector at the top if more than one session is shown.

Up Next

  • A countdown: Today, Tomorrow or In N days.
  • Your seat number, the course code and title.
  • The date and time, and the hall name and code.
  • Navigate to Hall: directions from where you are.

Upcoming and Completed

The progress bar shows how many papers are done ("2 / 6", "4 remaining"). Upcoming lists your later exams, each with its hall and seat. An exam moves to Completed once it has finished; when every paper is done you see "All exams completed".

Read hall and seat together

Seat numbers start at 1 in each hall. "Seat 14" in Block C Lecture Hall is a different seat from "Seat 14" in the Multipurpose Hall, so always check both.

Seat number not yet assigned?

If your hall is shown with the notice "Seat number not yet assigned", please contact the exams office.

4

Getting to your exam hall

Live walking directions from wherever you are on campus.

  1. Open the directionsTap Navigate to Hall on your timetable, or open the Navigate tab and tap Navigate on any hall.
  2. Allow location accessYour browser asks for permission the first time. The blue dot shows where you are; the green pin is the hall.
  3. Follow the routeThe green line is the walking route, with the distance and time (for example "850 m · 11 min walk"). It updates as you move; if the route service can't be reached, a straight line and an estimated walking time are shown instead.
  4. Prefer Google Maps?Tap Open in Google Maps for turn-by-turn walking directions. Tip: check the route the day before your first paper, and set off early.

Managing your courses

Keep your course list right, so your timetable is right.

The Courses tab lists the courses for your level in the selected session's semester. The session you pick is remembered on the Timetable tab too.

You seeWhat it means
+ EnrollYou are not taking this course yet. Tap to add it.
× DropYou are enrolled. Tap and confirm to remove it.
LockedThe timetable for this course is published, so it stays on your list.

At the bottom you can see how many courses you are enrolled in and your total credit units.

Good to know
  • If you enrol after the timetable has been generated, you are given a free seat automatically when there is room.
  • Dropping a course before publication frees your seat, and you can add the course again later.
  • Make your changes before the timetable is published.

Your profile

Your details, your avatar and your preferences.

The Profile tab shows your name, matric number, email, programme, level and department.

  • Choose an avatar: tap your picture or Change avatar and pick an emoji.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

My timetable is empty.

Your timetable appears once the exams office publishes it. Check that the right session is selected at the top, and that you are enrolled in your courses on the Courses tab.

I see "Seat number not yet assigned".

Your hall has been chosen, but a seat number has not been given yet. Please contact the exams office.

I registered in the wrong department or level.

Contact the exams office. They can correct your department, programme and level. Your sign-in stays the same; then check your courses on the Courses tab.

I forgot my password.

Contact the exams office. They can set a new password for you, which you then use to sign in.

Location isn't working.

Allow location access for the site in your browser settings and make sure your phone's location is switched on. The portal must be opened over a secure (https) address: use https://smart-exam-timetable.vercel.app. You can always tap Open in Google Maps.

Why can't I drop a course?

Courses whose timetable has been published show Locked and stay on your list. Speak to the exams office if something needs to change.

Can I use the portal on a computer?

Yes. It works in any modern browser, and the tabs are simply at the top of the screen instead of the bottom.
